AI Efficiency Monitor — Banking

Revenue per employee is the tell. If a bank is genuinely implementing AI, it shows up as more revenue carried by fewer people — and the market should pay a higher multiple for it. This monitor ranks the 50 largest US bank holding companies by that tell and names, first, the banks where implementation is already showing up in the numbers — and second, the ones standing still. Averages don't tell you who to watch; names do.
