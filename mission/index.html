The document, in its own words

Quoted verbatim from “Legal Justice AI Draft 32,” under the document’s own headings.

Vision

We aim to connect people to the legal industry by creating the largest human-centered legal services company, democratizing access to justice through AI, data, and services. We envision a future where legal processes are faster, more affordable, and accessible to all, breaking down the extraordinary barrier between people and justice, fostering a fairer society through constant innovation, and redefining the legal industry’s relationship with society.

Mission

Our mission is to pioneer a new industry at the intersection of AI and legal services, both creating and transforming legal support. Legal Justice AI offers 24/7 comprehensive assistance, empowering individuals to navigate litigation with greater awareness, confidence, less stress, and better outcomes. We make justice more accessible through a vast array of tools, information, data, analytics, planning, and, ultimately, advanced judicial capabilities.

Purpose

Legal Justice AI seeks to simplify access to legal information, empower individuals in their legal journeys, and reduce the overall burden of litigation on society. Our company prioritizes the well-being of our customers by providing equitable, data-driven legal insight and comprehensive services and building trust through an absolute dedication to customer care and success. We intend to steer the reimagination of the legal system into a more equitable, accessible system focused on problem-solving using various tools, including emerging AI superintelligence.
